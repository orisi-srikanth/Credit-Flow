Root Cause Analysis
Why traditional underwriting keeps failing MSMEs

The bank wasn't wrong to use CIBIL.
It was using the wrong tool for the wrong borrower.

🔄
The circular credit trap
You need a credit score to get a loan. You need a loan to get a credit score. First-time borrowers are permanently excluded by design.
📊
Bureau data is a proxy — not reality
CIBIL measures past borrowing behaviour. Ravi's GST returns, UPI inflows, and supplier payments prove he's creditworthy — but the bank has no system to read them.
⏳
Manual underwriting is too slow and too expensive
Processing a ₹2 lakh loan manually costs a bank ₹2,100+ in underwriting effort. The economics don't work — so banks ignore small-ticket MSME lending entirely.
